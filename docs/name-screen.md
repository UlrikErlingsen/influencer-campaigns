# Name screen: CreatorSignal → InfluenceSignal

Screen date: 1 October 2026.

## Result

**CreatorSignal has an exact-name conflict with an active product in an adjacent field.** Unlike TrackSignal's screen, this one found a live company using the exact name. Keep "CreatorSignal" as the internal working name only, and decide on the public name before release.

Findings from the screen:

- **creatorsignal.io — active product, exact name.** "CreatorSignal" is a live AI tool that validates YouTube video ideas for creators ("Go, Refine, or Kill" verdicts), with free sign-up, a pricing page, an API, and blog posts dated April 2026. No ™/® symbol was shown on the page. It sells to creators rather than to brands running campaigns, but both sit in the creator economy and would be found by the same searches.
- **"Creator Signal" (two words) attributed to Thematic** in one search-result snippet (feedback from creators for brands, labels and agencies). The linked page redirected to Thematic's app and did not mention it; **not confirmed**.
- **GitHub:** three small repositories use the name (`nee-vir/CreatorSignal`, `norbert351/creatorsignal`, `Shanthanreddy99/creatorsignal-chicago`; no stars, pushed in September 2026). No descriptions.
- **Package registries:** the exact names were open on PyPI (`creatorsignal`, `creator-signal`) and npm (`creatorsignal`) at screen time.
- "createSignal" (SolidJS, react-rxjs) dominates search results for the near spelling; it is an unrelated programming function.

## Decision

On 1 October 2026 Ulrik chose **InfluenceSignal**, and the project was renamed the same day (package `influencesignal`, environment variables `INFLUENCESIGNAL_*`, database file `influencesignal.db`). The repository name `influencer-campaigns` is unchanged. The findings above about CreatorSignal are kept as the record of why the name changed.

The formal checks under *Recommendation* below still apply to InfluenceSignal before commercial use.

## Practical assessment (CreatorSignal)

- **Direct exact-name conflict:** yes — creatorsignal.io, active, adjacent field.
- **Conceptual fit of CreatorSignal:** good, but shared with the conflicting product.
- **Discoverability:** poor; searches for the name lead to the other product and to `createSignal`.
- **Legal confidence:** low for every name above, because no formal trademark clearance was performed.

## Limits

This was a practical product-name screen, not legal advice or a trademark clearance opinion. It did not search official trademark registers (Patentstyret, EUIPO, WIPO), the Brønnøysund company register, domains and their history, app stores, social handles, translations, or every relevant class and jurisdiction. Search absence is not proof of availability.

## Recommendation

Rename before public release. **InfluenceSignal** was the cleanest candidate on this screen and fits the Signal suite pattern. Before adopting any name, check Patentstyret and EUIPO for exact and similar marks in classes 9 and 42 (software) and 35 (marketing services), check the Brønnøysund register, confirm the domain and social handles, repeat the GitHub/PyPI/npm searches, and obtain qualified legal advice where the name will carry material value.
